# Grand City Mobile — Church World Design

Grand City includes original churches as part of the city's civilian and community world. They are designed as fictional locations with original architecture, interiors, signage, landscaping, and gameplay systems.

## Church variants

- Grand Cathedral — landmark downtown church with a large sanctuary, bell tower, plaza, gardens, and visitor parking.
- Community Church — medium neighborhood church beside residential streets, with classrooms and a community hall.
- Modern Church — contemporary glass-and-stone building in Innovation District.
- Hillside Chapel — smaller peaceful chapel overlooking North Hills/Green Valley.
- Waterfront Chapel — compact coastal church with landscaped grounds and pedestrian access.

## Exterior

- Distinctive original facade and tower silhouettes
- Main entrance, side entrances, accessible ramps, steps, covered drop-off
- Bell tower and decorative architectural details
- Parking area, sidewalks, trees, benches, gardens, lighting, signage
- Night lighting with warm interior windows and restrained exterior illumination

## Interior

- Main sanctuary/worship hall
- Entrance lobby
- Seating areas
- Community/fellowship room
- Offices
- Classrooms/meeting rooms
- Restrooms
- Storage/service rooms
- Optional small library or counseling room

Interiors should be modular and streamable so distant churches do not consume unnecessary mobile memory or rendering cost.

## Gameplay hooks

Churches are civilian/community locations rather than combat spaces. They can support peaceful social events, community missions, charity/donation systems, NPC schedules, roleplay conversations, and server-authored public events. Any money, inventory, permissions, or rewards remain authoritative on the game server.

## Visual direction

Target realistic PBR materials, believable proportions, detailed but mobile-scalable interiors, varied vegetation, natural daylight, sunset, rainy weather, and atmospheric night lighting. Use original or properly licensed assets; real-world churches and other games are visual references only and should not be copied one-to-one.

## Mobile performance

- LODs for architecture and props
- HLOD/streaming for distant structures
- Instanced vegetation and repeated props
- Separate interior loading where practical
- Scalable shadow/reflection/effect quality
- High-detail hero cathedral reserved for nearby players
- Lower-cost distant silhouettes for the skyline
